2. GAME OVERVIEW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A node-busting incremental game based on the fantasy of hacking a corporate slot machine, making enough money to escape the "matrix"

Tired of making money for your corporate overlords while you get paid peanuts? 
Hack into your work computer and syphon cash straight from the slots and into 
your bank! In this corporate dystopia, jobs have been reduced to betting all 
you've got and letting the house (your boss) win. Now Hiring!

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KEY FEATURES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Increasingly insane slot machine action
• Unlocking different features and power-ups to create immense revenue
• Mix and match features to create different "builds" and optimise profit
• Beat your boss and flip the script on corporate life
• A short, tight gameplay experience

6. ABOUT DIGITAL UMAMI
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3 Bournemouth University graduates who started making games together. After development hell during a first
gamje development project, we decided to create Gamble Inc in under a year to prove to ourselves we could 
complete the full development cycle. The demo was created in less than 6 months. We want to bring a satirical 
twist to indie game design, and create games that we love.
